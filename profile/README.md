<h3 align="center">Label AI video. Even after it's reposted.</h3>

<p align="center">
Tectra hides an invisible, verifiable mark in AI-generated video that survives TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp and re-shares,<br>
and lets anyone check who made a copy, with a receipt they can verify without trusting us.
</p>
